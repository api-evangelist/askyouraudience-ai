Ask Your Audience (AYA) provides AI‑driven audience research allowing teams to test ideas, ads, and concepts before spending. Users can ask any question and receive rapid, human‑like responses from a curated panel, helping validate concepts, pricing, and messaging without lengthy recruitment. The platform emphasizes no‑sign‑up free trials, real interview‑based data, and integrates digital twins for deeper insights.

**Areas:** AI · AudienceResearch · MarketTesting · NoSignUp · DigitalTwins

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Ask Your Audience: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
